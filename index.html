TerraLens retrieves monthly point data from NASA POWER using community AG. Meteorological products are model/assimilation-derived, not direct station measurements. Point values represent gridded products, not province boundaries. Missing values remain gaps.

Annual means are weighted by calendar days. Rainfall rates are integrated into totals. Statistical trends describe history and do not predict future conditions.
